# GRUBSTAKE · 出资人

**Play: https://olivia-fsm.github.io/grubstake-play/**

A narrative game about learning to be a venture capitalist, on the island of Vantage. You have thirty-six months, a family
factory under a balloon loan, and no idea what a term sheet is. Learn from the people you meet, then choose: back
founders as an investor, or become one.

一个关于学会做风险投资人的叙事游戏。凡提奇岛上，三十六个月，一座背着气球贷款的家族工厂，而你连条款清单是什么都不知道。

- Plays in the browser on phones, tablets and laptops — English and 中文.
- **On a phone:** hold it sideways. To keep it like an app — iPhone: Safari ▸ Share ▸ *Add to Home Screen*;
  Android: *Install app*. After the first visit it also works offline.
- Your progress is saved in your own browser.

This repository holds only the built game. An educational game, not financial advice.
